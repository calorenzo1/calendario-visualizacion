Calendars, Economy & Gender Visualization

Interactive data visualization exploring the relationship between national calendar structures, economic development (GDP per capita), and female labor participation across countries (2010–2019).

Explore the visualizations

  • World Map — GDP per capita
  • Scatter — Calendar vs Economy vs Gender
  • GDP Evolution (Top countries)
  • Female Labor Participation Evolution
  • Calendar Intensity Evolution

Project overview

This project does not aim to establish causality, but to explore patterns and correlations between economic, social, and institutional variables using interactive data visualization.
